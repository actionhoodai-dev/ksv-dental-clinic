Save your natural teeth with our advanced, pain-free root canal procedures — modern techniques that make the experience comfortable and stress-free.

Saving Teeth, Eliminating Pain

A root canal becomes necessary when the soft tissue (pulp) inside your tooth becomes infected or inflamed due to deep decay, repeated dental procedures, or trauma. Without treatment, the infection can cause an abscess and may lead to tooth loss.

At KSV Dental Clinic, Dr. Suresh uses advanced endodontic techniques with modern rotary instruments, digital imaging, and effective local anesthesia to perform root canals that are virtually pain-free. Most procedures are completed in a single visit, and you can resume normal activities immediately.

The Procedure

What to Expect

1. Diagnosis & X-Ray

Digital X-rays reveal the extent of infection. Dr. Suresh examines the tooth and explains the treatment plan in detail.

2. Numbing & Access

Local anesthesia ensures you feel no pain. A small opening is made in the crown to access the infected pulp chamber.

3. Cleaning & Shaping

Infected pulp tissue is carefully removed. The canals are cleaned, shaped, and disinfected using rotary instruments.

4. Filling & Restoration

The cleaned canals are filled with biocompatible material and sealed. A crown may be placed for long-term protection.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

Modern root canal therapy is virtually painless. With effective anesthesia and advanced techniques, most patients report that the procedure is no more uncomfortable than getting a filling.

How long does it take?

Most root canals are completed in a single 45–60 minute appointment. Complex cases with multiple canals may require a follow-up visit.

How long does a root canal last?

With proper care and a quality crown, a root canal treated tooth can last a lifetime. Regular dental checkups help ensure the restoration remains intact.
