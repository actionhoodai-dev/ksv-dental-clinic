The Gold Standard for Missing Teeth

Dental implants are small titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone, where they serve as artificial tooth roots. Once healed, they provide a rock-solid foundation for custom-made crowns that look, feel, and function exactly like your natural teeth.

Unlike dentures or bridges, implants don't affect adjacent teeth and actually help preserve jawbone density. With a success rate of over 95%, dental implants are the most reliable long-term solution for tooth replacement.

Benefits

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Looks & Feels Natural

Custom-crafted crowns match the shape, color, and size of your natural teeth. No one will know the difference.

Preserves Jawbone

Titanium fuses with your jawbone through osseointegration, preventing bone loss that occurs with missing teeth.

Long-Lasting

With proper care, dental implants can last 25+ years — often a lifetime. They're the most durable tooth replacement option.

Eat Comfortably

Implants restore full chewing power. Enjoy all your favorite foods without worry, unlike removable dentures.

FAQs

Common Questions

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with good general health are suitable candidates. A consultation with Dr. Suresh including X-rays will help determine if implants are right for you.

How long does the implant process take?

The implant is placed in a single visit. However, osseointegration (bone fusion) takes 3–6 months before the final crown can be placed. During this time, a temporary crown is provided.

Is the procedure painful?

The implant placement is done under local anesthesia and is typically less uncomfortable than a tooth extraction. Post-operative discomfort is mild and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.
