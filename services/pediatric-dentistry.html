Making Dental Visits Fun, Not Scary

A child's first dental experience shapes their attitude toward dental care for life. At KSV Dental Clinic, Dr. Suresh is experienced in working with children, using gentle techniques, simple explanations, and a warm approach that puts even the most anxious child at ease.

We focus on preventive care and education, teaching children and parents about proper brushing, nutrition, and habits that protect developing teeth. Starting dental visits early — by age 1 or when the first tooth appears — sets the foundation for lifelong oral health.

Services

Children's Dental Services

First Visit & Exam

A gentle, friendly introduction to dental care. We examine developing teeth, check for early issues, and build trust with your child.

Fluoride Treatment

Protective fluoride application strengthens developing tooth enamel and provides resistance against cavities.

Dental Sealants

Thin protective coatings applied to back teeth (molars) to prevent cavities in the deep grooves where food easily gets trapped.

Habit Counseling

Guidance for parents on thumb-sucking, pacifier use, and diet habits that affect dental development and alignment.

FAQs

Common Questions

When should my child first visit the dentist?

We recommend a first visit by age 1 or within 6 months of the first tooth erupting. Early visits help establish a dental home and catch any issues early.

My child is scared of the dentist. What can I do?

This is very common! Dr. Suresh is experienced with anxious children. We use a "tell-show-do" approach, go at the child's pace, and make every visit positive. Many kids end up looking forward to their dental visits.

Are baby teeth really important?

Yes! Baby teeth hold space for permanent teeth, aid in speech development, and help with proper nutrition. Neglecting them can lead to infections, pain, and alignment problems later.
