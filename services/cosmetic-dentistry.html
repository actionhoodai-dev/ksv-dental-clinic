Cosmetic Services We Offer

Dental Veneers

Ultra-thin porcelain or composite shells that cover the front surface of teeth, correcting chips, stains, and uneven shapes instantly.

Dental Bonding

Tooth-colored resin applied directly to repair chips, cracks, and gaps. A quick, affordable way to enhance your smile in a single visit.

Tooth Reshaping

Subtle contouring of tooth enamel to improve shape, length, or surface irregularities. Produces instant, polished results with zero downtime.

FAQs

Common Questions

How long do veneers last?

Porcelain veneers typically last 10–15 years with proper care. Composite veneers last 5–7 years. Both can be maintained much longer with good oral hygiene.

Is cosmetic dentistry painful?

Most cosmetic procedures are minimally invasive with little to no discomfort. Veneers may require slight tooth preparation, done under local anesthesia for comfort.

Will cosmetic work look natural?

Absolutely. Dr. Suresh specializes in creating results that look completely natural. We carefully match shade, shape, and proportion to complement your facial features.
