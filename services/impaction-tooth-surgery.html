Impaction Tooth Surgery

Expert management of impacted wisdom teeth and complex extractions with precision surgery and pain-free care.

Surgical Solutions for Complex Cases

An impacted tooth is one that fails to fully erupt through the gum line, often causing pain, infection, or damage to adjacent teeth. Most commonly occurring with wisdom teeth, impactions require expert surgical intervention to prevent long-term complications.

At KSV Dental Clinic, Dr. L. Suresh performs these delicate procedures using advanced surgical techniques that prioritize patient comfort and rapid recovery.
