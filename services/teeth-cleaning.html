Professional Scaling & Polishing

Remove stubborn plaque and tartar buildup with professional cleaning — the easiest way to maintain healthy gums and prevent tooth decay.

Beyond What Brushing Can Reach

Even with diligent brushing and flossing, plaque and tartar accumulate in areas that are difficult to reach at home. Professional teeth cleaning removes these deposits, preventing cavities, gum disease, and bad breath.

Our scaling procedure uses ultrasonic instruments to gently and effectively remove hardened tartar both above and below the gumline. This is followed by a professional polish that leaves your teeth smooth, clean, and noticeably brighter.

Benefits

Why Professional Cleaning Matters

Prevents Gum Disease

Regular scaling removes tartar that causes gingivitis and periodontal disease — the leading cause of tooth loss in adults.

Fresher Breath

Bacterial buildup is a major cause of bad breath. Professional cleaning eliminates odor-causing bacteria from hard-to-reach areas.

Quick & Comfortable

A typical cleaning session takes 30–45 minutes. Ultrasonic scalers make the process efficient and comfortable.

Early Detection

During your cleaning visit, Dr. Suresh examines your teeth and gums, catching potential problems before they become serious.

FAQs

Common Questions

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

We recommend professional cleaning every 6 months. Patients with gum disease may benefit from more frequent cleanings every 3–4 months.

Does teeth cleaning hurt?

Most patients experience little to no discomfort. You may feel slight sensitivity during tartar removal, but the procedure is generally painless. Anesthesia is available for sensitive patients.

Does scaling damage teeth?

No. Professional scaling is completely safe and does not damage tooth enamel. It removes harmful deposits that would otherwise damage your teeth and gums over time.
