Root Canal Treatment

Expert root canal therapy aimed at saving your natural tooth with minimal discomfort and maximum durability.

Painless & Effective Results

Root canal treatment is essential for saving a tooth that is severely decayed or infected. Our clinic uses digital apex locators and advanced rotary endodontics to ensure a precise and painless experience.

With over 30 years of experience, Dr. L. Suresh ensures high success rates for complex root canal cases, often completing the procedure in a single visit.
